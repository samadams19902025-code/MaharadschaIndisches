Our Story

Not fusion.
The real thing.

Authentic Indian cooking — nothing toned down, nothing reinvented for unfamiliar palates.

At Maharadscha, we cook the way it's cooked in India. Whole spices, ground generously. A tandoor that runs hot through lunch and dinner. Naan shaped by hand and lentils that simmer for hours.

And the heat is yours to choose — from gentle and fragrant to genuinely fiery. Just tell us how brave you're feeling. Vegetarian, vegan and meat dishes share the same table, because traditional Indian cooking has always celebrated vegetables.
